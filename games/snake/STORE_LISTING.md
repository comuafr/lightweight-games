## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Snake is the game you already know — and nothing you don't.

Swipe to steer. Eat the food. Grow longer. Don't hit the walls or your own tail.
Beat your best score, then beat it again. That's the whole game, and it's all
here.

WHY THIS ONE
• Tiny download — under a megabyte. It installs in a blink and barely touches
  your storage.
• Truly offline — no internet permission at all. Play on a plane, on the subway,
  anywhere.
• No ads. No pop-ups. No "watch a video to continue". Ever.
• No tracking, no accounts, no data collected. What happens on your phone stays
  on your phone.
• No permissions requested. None.
• Pay once. It's yours. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up, down, left or right to turn the snake.
• Each piece of food makes you longer and adds to your score.
• Crash into a wall or yourself and it's game over — tap to try again.
• Your best score is saved on your device.

Built to be small and fair: pure, lightweight code with no game engine and no
third-party libraries. It runs smoothly on phones old and new (Android 7 and
up).

If you miss when games were simple, instant, and respectful of your phone and
your attention — this is for you.

Light Games is a pocket arcade that works everywhere: on the train, in the car, on a plane, or anywhere the signal is bad. Fifteen small games in one tiny app, with nothing to set up.

FREE
• Gobbler: guide a growing snake to the apples without biting your own tail. There are no walls: leave one edge and you come back on the other.
• Fusion: slide numbered tiles to merge matching ones and build the biggest number.
• Wall Smash: bounce the ball off your paddle and break every brick. Ten levels with tough bricks and steel blocks, bonus and malus capsules, and multi-ball.

ONE PURCHASE UNLOCKS TWELVE MORE
• Color Echo: repeat a sequence of colors that gets longer every round.
• Twins: flip cards and find the matching pairs.
• Tile Shuffle: slide the tiles back into order.
• Three in Line: line up three marks before your opponent does.
• Paddles: a duel of paddles and a ball, for one or two players.
• Drop Four: drop discs and line up four of your color.
• Chirp: tap to flap through the gaps. The further you go, the tighter it gets.
• Hidden Bombs: clear the field without touching a bomb.
• Secret Word: guess the word letter by letter before you run out of tries.
• Flip Discs: trap your opponent's discs between yours to flip them to your color.
• Nine by Nine: fill the grid with digits, none repeated in a row, a column or a box.
• Pixel Reveal: follow the number clues to uncover a hidden picture.

WHY YOU WILL LIKE IT
• No ads, no pop-ups, no fake buttons, no subscription. Pay once, play forever.
• No account, no tracking, no data collection. The app never needs the network, except Google Play itself when you buy.
• Tiny download, instant launch, gentle on the battery.
• Big touch targets and very little text, so young children can play on their own. Every game has a short built-in help screen.
• Several games are for two players on the same screen.
• Your best scores are saved on your device.
• A parental gate (hold the button for 3 seconds) protects the purchase.
